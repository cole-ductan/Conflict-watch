Iran's seven conditions

Iran's 'seven conditions' — a labeled reconstruction, not an official list

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Diplomacy / Conditions · Thursday, November 12, 2026 at 2:00 AM CT

In plain English

Iran keeps talking about “seven conditions” for ending the war — things like ending the US naval blockade and lifting sanctions. But no official numbered list exists; the version going around is pieced together by news outlets from themes Iranian officials have raised.

Full brief

Iranian officials have repeatedly referred to seven conditions for ending the war, but no verbatim numbered official list has been published, and no $30bn figure appears in reputable reporting of the conditions. The list below is a reconstruction from the themes multiple outlets report — it is not a quoted official text. The themes: (1) end the war on all fronts, including Lebanon and Yemen; (2) full lifting of the US naval blockade; (3) release of frozen Iranian assets (amount unspecified in the list itself); (4) lifting of sanctions, including oil sanctions; (5) withdrawal of US forces from the region / an end to US threats and interference; (6) war reparations; (7) recognition of the Iran–Oman maritime corridor / an end to US attacks on Iranian territory. Grouping and wording vary by outlet.

⚠ Reconstruction — not a quoted official list; no $30bn figure
Confidence

High that no verbatim official numbered list exists in reputable reporting — outlets presenting the conditions describe them as a reconstruction. The $12bn figure sometimes attached to the conditions belongs to the Sept 25, 2026 seven-day roadmap (see that report), not to this list.

Hodeidah Port Struck

Yemen's Houthis threaten to target Jeddah and other Saudi Red Sea ports

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Coalition airstrikes — Yemen / Red Sea · Tuesday, November 24, 2026 at 8:46 PM CT

In plain English

The Saudi-led coalition bombed Yemen's Hodeidah port overnight after warning civilians to evacuate, hitting port facilities and targets in two northern provinces. The Houthis answered by threatening Jeddah and every Saudi Red Sea port. No casualties were reported.

Full brief

What happened

After Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said Jeddah and all Saudi Red Sea ports would be targeted if the coalition struck Hodeidah, the Saudi-led coalition said it targeted operational capabilities at Hodeidah port and destroyed a number of targets in the Yemeni governorates of Saada and Amran (Reuters, coalition statement, Wednesday Nov 25). Maritime security firm Vanguard Tech counted at least six airstrikes on Hodeidah on 25 November, following the coalition's evacuation warning to civilians (Seatrade Maritime News). The extent of damage to port infrastructure, the impact on vessels alongside, and the operational status of the port remain unclear. Contested / unverified: Houthi media claimed the Hodeidah airport was also hit — unverified, attributed only, no confirmation.

Context

this follows the coalition's second evacuation warning for the port in four days, already on this dashboard.

Why this matters for the US

Hodeidah sits on the Red Sea shipping chokepoint and the exchange puts Saudi Red Sea ports — and the trade routes US forces in the region help protect — in the line of fire, days after a Houthi strike injured 11 in Riyadh.

⚠ Damage to port infrastructure and the port's operational status remain unclear; the Houthi-media claim that Hodeidah airport was also hit is unverified — attributed only, no confirmation.
Confidence

High on the strikes and the threats (Reuters + coalition statement + Vanguard Tech); the airport-hit claim is unverified.

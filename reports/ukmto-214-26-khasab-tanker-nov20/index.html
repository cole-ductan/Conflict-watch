Tanker Struck Again

Fire reported on tanker after projectile strike off Oman, UKMTO says (Reuters, Nov 20, 2026)

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime attack — Strait of Hormuz · Friday, November 20, 2026 at 9:00 AM CT

In plain English

A tanker sailing out of the Strait of Hormuz was hit by an unknown projectile on its port bow on Friday morning, about 8 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman. A fire broke out on board, but no one was hurt and there was no environmental damage. UKMTO has not named the ship or said who fired — and no one has claimed the attack. It is the third tanker struck north of Khasab since Monday, and the eighth attack on shipping in the strait and the Arabian Gulf in the last week.

Full brief

What happened

UKMTO Warning 214-26: a tanker on outbound transit through the Strait of Hormuz was struck on the port bow by an unknown projectile at 15:00 UTC on Friday, Nov 20 (9:00 AM CT), about 8 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman. The vessel's company security officer reported a fire on board. No casualties and no environmental impact were reported; authorities are investigating. UKMTO issued the warning at 16:03 UTC.

Context

this is the third tanker struck north of Khasab since Monday, after the ADNOC vessel NAVIG8 MESSI (Nov 17, 13 NM north of Khasab, one crew member missing, 11 injured) and the Nov 18 engine-room strike (6 NM north of Khasab, Warning 212-26, crew safe). UKMTO has reported eight attacks on shipping in the strait and the Arabian Gulf in the last week.

Contested claims

none attached to this incident: UKMTO says 'unknown projectile' and has not attributed the strike. Iran's separate claim that tankers hit naval mines is unverified and is not linked by any source to this incident; that claim family is tracked separately in the Unverified section.

Why this matters for the US

The Strait of Hormuz is the chokepoint for Gulf oil and a US Navy operating area. A third tanker hit in four days in the same waters — even with no casualties — keeps the pressure on US and allied shipping, pushes up energy and insurance costs, and raises the chance of a miscalculation that pulls US forces in. The pattern, not any single hit, is the threat.

⚠ Attacker unidentified — vessel unnamed by UKMTO; no claim of responsibility.
Confidence

High for the UKMTO facts (warning number 214-26, 8 NM north of Khasab, unknown projectile on the port bow, fire on board, no casualties, no environmental impact — UKMTO's own warning, posted 16:03 UTC Nov 20 and reported by Reuters). Medium on the extent of the damage — UKMTO has not said how bad the fire is or whether the vessel can continue. No attribution — UKMTO has not said who fired and no group has claimed the attack.

Tehran warns “dark age”

Ghalibaf warns of a 'dark age'; Rezaei — not Ghalibaf — tells the US to 'surrender'

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Statements / Rhetoric · Monday, November 9, 2026 at 12:49 PM CT

In plain English

Iran's parliament speaker warns America and its allies are headed for a “dark age” unless Washington drops its “Israel First” mindset. A different Iranian official, Mohsen Rezaei, is the one who told the US to “surrender” — an earlier version of this report credited the wrong man, and that has been corrected.

Full brief

Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf posted on X on Nov 9: “Unless Washington drops the 'Israel First' mindset, America and its allies are headed straight for a dark age.” He also wrote that Israelis are working to drag the US into 'an endless war with Iran, a quagmire that no president… could escape.' Ghalibaf did NOT use the word 'surrender.' That word was Mohsen Rezaei's, also on X on Nov 9: “Time to SURRENDER to the seven conditions Iran has repeatedly set out.” Earlier versions of this archive attributed 'surrender' to Ghalibaf; that attribution was wrong and has been corrected.

⚠ Corrected Nov 12: 'surrender' reassigned from Ghalibaf to Rezaei
Confidence

High — both X posts are quoted verbatim by multiple outlets (Press TV, Middle East Monitor, ABNA for Ghalibaf; Fox News and others quoting @ir_rezaee for Rezaei).

Hodeidah Evacuation Warning

Saudi-led coalition issues evacuation warning for Yemen's Hodeidah port

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi missiles — Saudi Arabia · Tuesday, November 24, 2026 at 4:15 PM CT

In plain English

The Saudi-led coalition has told civilians to leave Yemen's port of Hodeidah, warning of a possible strike on the area — the second such warning in four days. The Houthis answered by threatening to hit Jeddah and every other Saudi port on the Red Sea, and told workers and shipping companies to clear out. Houthi-run media say the coalition has already attacked the port, but no independent source has confirmed that. Hodeidah is the main port bringing food and supplies into Houthi-held northern Yemen.

Full brief

What happened

The Saudi-led coalition issued an evacuation warning for Yemen's Hodeidah port on Tuesday, Nov 24, posting a map of the port area on X and asking civilians to leave 'for their safety,' Reuters reported, signaling a potential strike. It is the second evacuation warning for the port in four days, after the coalition struck Hodeidah on Friday, Nov 20. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree responded on X, saying Jeddah and all Saudi ports on the Red Sea coast would become targets if the coalition carries out its threat, warning port workers to evacuate immediately and urging shipping companies to move their ships out. The Houthis' Saba news agency reported shortly afterward that the coalition had launched an attack on Hodeidah port.

Context

Hodeidah is the main gateway for food and humanitarian supplies into Houthi-controlled northern Yemen, handling roughly 70 percent of the country's commercial imports, and the coalition says the port is used to smuggle weapons and ballistic missiles. The exchange follows the Nov 20 strike and the Houthis' Nov 19 strike on Riyadh's tallest tower.

Contested claims

the Saba report of a coalition attack on the port has not been independently confirmed; no coalition statement or independent outlet has confirmed a strike. The Houthis' threat to hit Saudi ports is a stated threat, not an attack that has happened.

Why this matters for the US

the Houthis are threatening Jeddah — the Red Sea's second-largest maritime hub — and every Saudi Red Sea port, putting a major US trade route and the ports of a close US ally at risk, next to US personnel and facilities in the kingdom.

⚠ Houthi claim of a coalition attack on Hodeidah port is unverified; the port threat is a stated threat, not a strike that has happened.
Confidence

High that the coalition issued the evacuation warning (Reuters, reporting the coalition's own post on X) and that the Houthis threatened Saudi Red Sea ports (Saree's own post on X, reported by Al Jazeera). The Saba claim of a coalition attack on the port is unverified — no independent confirmation.

Bahrain threat alerts

Bahrain issues first threat alerts since September as IRGC claims six drones hit US radar at Al-Rifaa base

highVerifiedIran / Gulf · Missiles / Drones — Bahrain · Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 10:25 PM CT

In plain English

Bahrain has issued its first nationwide threat alerts since September, telling people to stay calm and move to a safe place. Iran's Revolutionary Guards claim they sent six drones at a US radar station at a helicopter base in Bahrain. Bahrain's military says its air defenses intercepted the Iranian projectiles before they reached their targets, and blasts were heard in Manama. CENTCOM spokesman Tim Hawkins told The Washington Post there had been “no impacts to American forces” from Sunday's attacks — but he did not say whether radar equipment or other property was damaged, so the IRGC's radar-hit claim remains unconfirmed. Bahrain's Foreign Ministry condemned the attacks, held Iran fully responsible, and asked the UN Security Council to condemn them and take “firm” measures.

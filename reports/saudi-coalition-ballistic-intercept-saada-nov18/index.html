Ballistic Missile Intercepted

Coalition says it intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile aimed at southern Saudi Arabia and destroyed its launcher in Saada (Nov 18, 2026)

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi missiles — Saudi Arabia · Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 4:06 PM CT

In plain English

The Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile aimed at Saudi Arabia's southern region on Wednesday evening, then destroyed the launcher in Saada, Yemen. Saudi civil defence issued alerts in Abha and Khamis Mushait and lifted them minutes later. No damage or casualties were reported, and the Houthis have not commented.

Full brief

What happened

The Saudi-led coalition's spokesman said on X at 22:06 UTC on Wednesday, Nov 18, that Saudi air defences had intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile launched by the Houthis toward the southern region of Saudi Arabia, and at 22:47 UTC that the coalition had destroyed the ballistic missile launcher in Saada, Yemen, that it said was used for the launch. Saudi civil defence had issued alerts in Abha and Khamis Mushait at 21:49 and 21:50 UTC and lifted them about six minutes later, without naming the threat.

Context

this is the third Houthi air attack on Saudi Arabia reported on Wednesday, Nov 18, after two cruise missiles fired at Riyadh in the morning and two attack drones over Riyadh and the southern region in the afternoon — both already on this dashboard. It follows the coalition's Tuesday strikes on 60 Houthi military targets across Yemen, also already on this dashboard.

Contested claims

the interception and the launcher strike are the coalition's account only: no independent confirmation of wreckage or damage has been published, neither statement gives the place or time of the interception or a precise location for the launcher, and the Houthis have not announced the launch.

Why this matters for the US

A ballistic missile — a heavier weapon than the cruise missiles and drones intercepted earlier the same day — was aimed at the southern region around Khamis Mushait, home to King Khalid Air Base, where US forces operate. The Houthis are sustaining near-daily missile and drone fire at a key US partner while Iran attacks US bases in the Gulf.

⚠ Coalition account only — no independent verification of the interception or the launcher strike; the Houthis have not announced the launch.
Confidence

High that the coalition made the announcement and that Saudi civil defence issued and lifted alerts in Abha and Khamis Mushait (coalition spokesman's X posts; civil defence posts). The interception and launcher destruction themselves are the coalition's account — no independent verification published, and the Houthis have not commented.

Shots Fired Off Yemen

Armed skiff fires shots at bulk carrier off Yemen, UKMTO says (Safety4sea, Nov 20, 2026)

lowVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime security — Gulf of Aden · Friday, November 20, 2026 at 5:02 AM CT

In plain English

A small boat carrying six armed men fired four shots at a large cargo ship in the Gulf of Aden, off Yemen, on Friday morning. The ship's own security team fired warning shots back, the small boat moved away, and the ship carried on with no one hurt and no damage. The British maritime agency UKMTO logged the incident as suspicious activity. Nobody has claimed responsibility, and the men in the boat have not been identified.

Full brief

What happened

A dark blue skiff carrying six unidentified armed individuals approached the port side of an eastbound bulk carrier in the Internationally Recommended Transit Corridor, about 95 nautical miles southwest of Al Mukalla, Yemen, at about 11:02 UTC on Friday, Nov 20 (5:02 AM CT), UKMTO said. Four shots were fired in quick succession from the skiff; the vessel's armed security team returned warning shots, the skiff moved away, and the bulk carrier continued its voyage. The master confirmed no crew members were injured and the vessel sustained no damage. UKMTO issued Warning 213-26 and classified the incident as suspicious activity.

Context

The Gulf of Aden is the approach to the Bab el-Mandeb strait and the Red Sea, one of the world's busiest shipping lanes. The incident comes two days after the Nov 18 tanker attack off Khasab, but UKMTO has not linked it to any group, and no party has claimed it. Armed skiff approaches in these waters have historically included both piracy and Houthi harassment, and the two cannot be told apart from this report alone.

Why this matters for the US

The Gulf of Aden carries a large share of US-bound trade and is patrolled by US and allied naval forces. Shots fired at a merchant ship there — even with no damage — are a direct threat to that trade route, and to the US forces tasked with keeping it open.

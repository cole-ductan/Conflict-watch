Russian Drone Enters Poland

Russian unmanned aerial vehicle briefly violated Polish airspace, Polish armed forces say

mediumVerifiedRussia–Ukraine · NATO airspace — Poland · Monday, November 23, 2026 at 5:20 PM CT

In plain English

A Russian drone briefly crossed into Polish airspace during Russia's overnight attack on western Ukraine, Poland's armed forces said on Monday. Poland had permission to shoot it down but held fire after the drone turned back east, because shooting it down could have endangered civilians. It is the second Russian airspace violation over Poland in three days, after three cruise missiles landed there on Friday.

Full brief

What happened

During Russia's overnight aerial attack on targets in western Ukraine (the night of November 22–23, 2026), a Russian unmanned aerial vehicle briefly violated Polish airspace, Poland's Armed Forces Operational Command said on X on Monday, November 23 (Reuters, Nov 23; Liveuamap records the same Operational Command statement). Polish forces were authorised to shoot the object down, but after it changed course and headed east, commanders decided to refrain from using weapons, citing the potential threat to civilians and the possible consequences of shooting it down.

Context

This is the second Russian airspace violation over Poland in three days: on Friday, November 20, three Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles landed in southeastern Poland during a massive Russian attack on Ukraine, and NATO responded on Monday by agreeing to deploy an AWACS early-warning aircraft. The overnight attack that produced this new violation involved 153 Russian strike drones, 134 of which Ukrainian air defences downed or suppressed, and killed at least 15 people in the Kyiv region and Zaporizhzhia. No injuries or damage were reported in Poland from the drone incursion.

Why this matters for the US

Poland is a NATO treaty ally hosting US forces, and Russian weapons have now crossed into allied airspace twice in three days — a pattern of spillover that tests the air defences the US is committed to help defend.

⚠ No weapons were fired and no damage or injuries were reported in Poland; the drone turned east on its own and Polish forces held fire.
Confidence

High on the violation itself — Reuters wire quoting the Polish Armed Forces Operational Command's own statement on X, corroborated by Liveuamap's record of the same statement. Medium on which night's attack it accompanied — the Operational Command tied it to the overnight attack on western Ukraine, which matches the November 22–23 overnight wave (153 drones).

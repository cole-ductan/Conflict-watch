Riyadh Missiles Downed

Saudi-led coalition intercepts two Houthi ballistic missiles launched toward Riyadh

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi missiles — Saudi Arabia · Wednesday, November 25, 2026 at 9:47 PM CT

In plain English

Early Thursday morning, the Saudi-led coalition said its air defenses shot down two ballistic missiles fired by Yemen's Houthis toward Riyadh, the Saudi capital. The coalition's spokesman announced the intercepts on X and gave no casualty or damage figures. The coalition also said it shot down two drones over the Eastern Province, and that a Houthi projectile hit Jazan Port on Wednesday, causing minor damage but no injuries.

Full brief

What happened

The Saudi-led coalition said early Thursday, Nov 26, that its air defenses intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched by Yemen's Houthis toward Riyadh, coalition spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki al-Maliki said in a post on X, reported by Muslim Network TV and Al Arabiya English. Al-Maliki gave no casualty or damage figures for the Riyadh intercepts; Saudi Civil Defense had issued an alert for the Riyadh region and lifted it minutes later. Al-Maliki also said coalition air defenses intercepted and destroyed two drones over Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province in the same overnight announcement.

Context

The coalition said on Wednesday it had shot down a Houthi drone near Jeddah Islamic Port, destroyed a cruise missile launched toward western Saudi Arabia, and that a Houthi projectile struck Jazan Port in the southwest, causing minor damage but no civilian injuries. The Houthis claimed the port attacks as retaliation for the coalition's strikes on Hodeidah port in Yemen, already on this dashboard.

Contested claims

the missile count and the interception outcome are the coalition's own account, not independently verified; the Houthis have not claimed the Riyadh missile launches.

Why this matters for the US

Riyadh hosts the US Embassy and American personnel, and the Houthis keep firing ballistic missiles at the Saudi capital in a week that has already seen its tallest tower hit twice — while the US State Department warned Americans in the region on Tuesday of a heightened threat from Iran-backed Houthis.

⚠ The coalition did not say where the missiles were launched from or where debris fell; the interception outcome is the coalition's own account. The Houthis have not claimed the Riyadh missile launches.
Confidence

High — the coalition spokesman announced the intercepts himself on X, and Al Arabiya English and Sky News Arabia carried the same announcement; the missile count and interception outcome are the coalition's account and are not independently verified. No casualties or damage were reported for the Riyadh intercepts.

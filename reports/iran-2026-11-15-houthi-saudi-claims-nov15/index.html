Houthis claim Saudi strikes

Houthis claim November 15 strikes on Riyadh airport, financial center and East-West Pipeline pumping stations; Saudi reports interceptions near Mecca and Medina (ISW Iran Update, November 15, 2026)

low-mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi claims — Saudi Arabia · Sunday, November 15, 2026 at 4:20 PM CT

In plain English

The Houthis claimed new strikes on Saudi targets on Sunday — two ballistic missiles at Riyadh's King Khalid airport and a financial center, plus two drones at East-West oil pipeline pumping stations. Saudi Arabia said its air defenses intercepted drones near Mecca and Medina the same day, but those are not the places the Houthis claimed to hit, and no damage from the claimed strikes has been confirmed. The claims have not been independently verified.

Full brief

The Houthi movement claimed on November 15 that it launched two ballistic missiles at King Khalid International Airport and a “financial center” in Riyadh, and two drones at two East-West Pipeline pumping stations, as part of its campaign to compel Saudi Arabia to end military operations in Yemen. Saudi Joint Forces Command spokesperson Major General Turki al-Maliki announced on November 15 that Saudi forces intercepted two drones near Mecca and two drones near Medina; he had announced on November 14 that Saudi forces intercepted three drones in Eastern Province and two ballistic missiles over Riyadh — interceptions covered in a separate report on this dashboard. These November 15 claims are distinct from the Houthi claims of November 13 already on this dashboard, which named the same target set on a different day.

Why this matters for the US

Saudi Arabia is a key US partner and the East-West Pipeline is an alternate oil route while Iran disrupts Hormuz; Houthi claims on Saudi critical infrastructure — verified or not — are part of the pressure campaign on the US and its Gulf partners.

⚠ Claimed strikes unverified — no damage confirmed at the claimed Riyadh or pipeline targets
Confidence

High the Houthis made these claims (Houthi Telegram channel, reported by the Institute for the Study of War) and High that Saudi Arabia announced the November 15 interceptions near Mecca and Medina (Saudi Joint Forces Command spokesperson). Low that any claimed strike hit its target or caused damage — ISW states it cannot verify the Houthis' claims, and the Saudi interceptions announced on November 15 were near Mecca and Medina, not the claimed Riyadh airport, financial center, or pipeline targets. The Houthis deny targeting Mecca and Medina.

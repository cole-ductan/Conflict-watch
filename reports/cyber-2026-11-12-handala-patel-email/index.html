FBI chief's email hacked

Iran-based Handala Hack Team claims hack of FBI Director Kash Patel's personal email

mediumVerifiedCyber / Homeland · Cyber / FBI · Thursday, November 12, 2026 at 9:30 AM CT

In plain English

Hackers got into FBI Director Kash Patel's personal email and posted photos and old documents online. The FBI says no classified or government systems were touched. A group called Handala, based in Iran, claims it did it — the FBI has not blamed any country.

Full brief

The FBI confirmed “malicious actors” targeted Director Kash Patel's personal email account; the Iran-based Handala Hack Team claimed responsibility and posted photos and documents online. No classified systems were breached and no government or classified data was compromised — the stolen material was personal (photos, travel details, older messages from about 2011–2022). The US State Department offers up to $10 million for information identifying Handala members. The FBI did NOT attribute the attack to a specific country; Handala's claim is the group's own claim, not independently verified attribution.

⚠ FBI did not attribute to a country — Handala claim is unverified attribution
Confidence

High that the FBI confirmed the targeting and that Handala claimed it; Low to Medium that Handala is state-directed.

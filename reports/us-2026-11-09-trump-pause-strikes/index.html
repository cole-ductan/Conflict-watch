Strikes paused, blockade stays

Trump pauses new major strikes on Iran 'for the time being' — blockade stays; explicitly not a ceasefire

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Strikes / Pause · Monday, November 9, 2026 at 2:00 PM CT

In plain English

President Trump says the US will hold off on new major strikes on Iran “for the time being,” after Gulf countries asked him to. The naval blockade of Iran's ports stays in place, and Trump stresses this is not a ceasefire — US forces stay “locked and loaded.”

Full brief

After meeting Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani at the White House on Nov 9, President Trump said the US would hold off on new/fresh major strikes on Iran 'for the time being,' at the behest of Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain and Kuwait — but the US naval blockade of Iranian ports stays in place. This is explicitly NOT a ceasefire: Trump said US forces remain 'locked and loaded' and ready to resume strikes.

⚠ Pause, not a ceasefire — blockade continues
Confidence

High that Trump made the statement as reported (AnewZ Nov 10; Reuters via HeadlinesXtra Nov 10). The pause is a stated intention, not an agreement — Iran has not accepted it as a ceasefire and the blockade continues.

Refinery Worker Killed

Saudi-led coalition says Houthis attacked Aramco's Jazan refinery

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi strike — Saudi Arabia · Thursday, November 26, 2026 at 12:08 PM CT

In plain English

A Houthi projectile hit Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery in southwestern Saudi Arabia on Thursday, killing one Nepalese worker and injuring several others, the Saudi Energy Ministry said. The strike started a fire that was brought under control and temporarily shut down some of the refinery's operations. The Saudi-led coalition said the Houthis carried out the attack and that it later destroyed the missile launch platform in Yemen's Hodeidah port used in the strike.

Full brief

What happened

A hostile aerial projectile struck Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery in southwestern Saudi Arabia, near the Yemeni border, on Thursday, November 26, 2026, the Saudi Energy Ministry said in a statement carried by the Saudi Press Agency. The strike killed one Nepalese worker and injured several people of different nationalities, started a fire that was brought under control, and temporarily halted some operations at the refinery, which is designed to process up to 400,000 barrels of crude oil per day. The Saudi-led coalition said earlier Thursday that Yemen's Houthis had attacked the refinery, and said it later destroyed a ballistic missile launch platform in the Yemeni port city of Hodeidah that it said the Houthis had used in the attack.

Context

The strike came a day after the Houthis claimed attacks on Ras Tanura, Saudi Arabia's largest refinery, and the Red Sea ports of Jeddah and Jazan; the coalition said it intercepted four drones over Ras Tanura and destroyed a drone near Jeddah Islamic Port. The Houthis have launched missiles and drones at Saudi airports, oil facilities and office buildings since fighting reignited in July, in a spillover from the US-Iran war.

Contested claims

the coalition attributes the strike to the Houthis; the Houthis have not been reported claiming this specific refinery strike, and no independent verification of the projectile type or launch point has been published.

Why this matters for the US

Saudi Arabia is a key US partner and the Jazan refinery is Red Sea energy infrastructure — a confirmed fatal strike on it shows the Houthi campaign against Saudi oil is now killing workers and shutting down operations, raising the risk to US personnel and energy interests in the kingdom.

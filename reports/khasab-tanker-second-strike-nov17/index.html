Tanker Hit Twice

Crew of tanker unaccounted for after second attack north of Oman's Khasab, UKMTO says

highVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime attack — Strait of Hormuz · Tuesday, November 17, 2026 at 3:48 AM CT

In plain English

The same tanker that was disabled by a strike on Monday off the coast of Oman, in the Strait of Hormuz, was hit again on Tuesday in the same spot, and its crew — reported safe after the first hit — is now unaccounted for. UKMTO, the UK maritime agency, says a fire broke out on board and the extent of the damage is unknown. Nobody has said who attacked it, and the ship has not been named.

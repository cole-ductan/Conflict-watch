China drills near Taiwan

China stages large, unannounced Pacific drills in 'silent intimidation' of Taiwan

mediumVerifiedChina–Taiwan · Exercises / Pacific · Friday, November 13, 2026 at 4:00 AM CT

In plain English

China held a huge, unannounced military exercise around Taiwan and across the western Pacific, with more than 100 ships at one point, according to a Taiwan security official. Taiwan calls it “silent intimidation.” The numbers come from Taiwan officials and have not been independently confirmed.

Full brief

Reuters (Taipei, Nov 13) reported that China carried out a large, unannounced military exercise across the western Pacific and around Taiwan in early November. A senior Taiwan security official said China's military and coast guard at one point massed more than 100 vessels; the drills included simulated attacks on other militaries and cities including Taiwan's; and a Chinese submarine launched an intercontinental ballistic missile from the Yellow Sea into the Chinese mainland during the drills. The deployment was larger than “Justice Mission 2025,” China's last formally named Taiwan drills nearly a year ago. The scale has decreased this week: around 70 vessels operating within the first island chain on Friday, and the Liaoning carrier strike group remains operating east of the Philippines. Xi Jinping gave a major Taiwan policy speech Thursday urging peaceful “reunification,” with no mention of force. China's defence ministry did not respond to a request for comment; China calls the carrier deployment “routine” exercises not directed at any country. The vessel counts and the submarine ICBM launch are attributed to anonymous Taiwan security officials and are not independently verified by Reuters.

⚠ Vessel counts and ICBM launch are Taiwan-official claims — not independently verified

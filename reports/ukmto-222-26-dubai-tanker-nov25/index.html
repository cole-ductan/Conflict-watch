Blast Near Dubai Tanker

UKMTO reports explosion near tanker west of Dubai

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime incident — Persian Gulf · Wednesday, November 25, 2026 at 3:30 PM CT

In plain English

A tanker captain reported an explosion from an unknown projectile just off his ship about 50 nautical miles west of Dubai on Wednesday, the UK's maritime agency (UKMTO) said. The blast caused no damage, no one was hurt, and there was no spill — but it shows the attacks on Gulf shipping have now reached the waters off the UAE's busiest port, well outside the Strait of Hormuz.

Full brief

What happened

The master of a tanker reported an explosion caused by an unknown projectile in close proximity to the vessel about 50 nautical miles west of Dubai, UAE, at 21:30 GMT on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations centre (UKMTO) said in Warning 222-26, classified as suspicious activity. UKMTO said the crew are safe and accounted for, with no damage to the tanker and no environmental impact, and advised vessels to transit with caution while authorities investigate; it did not name the vessel or say who fired the projectile (Reuters, Nov 25; Anadolu, Nov 25).

Context

It is the latest in a week in which at least ten tankers have been reported struck in and around the Strait of Hormuz, including three hit by projectiles in the strait earlier this week (see that report), and it extends the threat zone beyond the strait into the central Gulf off the UAE coast. Iran's Revolutionary Guard (IRGC) Navy has separately claimed tankers struck naval mines in the strait — those claims are unverified and are carried as separate Unverified cards; UKMTO's account of this incident says unknown projectile, not mines, and reports no damage.

Why this matters for the US

the waters off Dubai sit on the energy-shipping route the US Navy helps protect and serve the UAE, a key US partner hosting American forces — a projectile detonating beside a tanker there shows the shipping campaign now reaches the Gulf's main commercial approaches, not just the strait.

⚠ UKMTO did not identify the tanker or attribute the explosion to any party; the projectile type and who fired it are unknown. No damage, casualties, or environmental impact were reported.
Confidence

High that an explosion from an unknown projectile occurred near a tanker 50 NM west of Dubai at 21:30 GMT on Nov 25, with crew safe and no damage — UKMTO Warning 222-26, carried by Reuters and Anadolu. The vessel was not identified and no party has been blamed; who fired the projectile is unknown.

Assassination Plot Foiled

Denmark says it foiled Russian plot to assassinate US-Ukrainian rapper

mediumVerifiedRussia–Ukraine · Russian hybrid operation — Denmark · November 20, 2026 · time not stated by the source

In plain English

Danish police say they stopped a Russian intelligence plot to kill a US-Ukrainian rapper before a concert near Copenhagen in September. Two men are charged with attempted murder and helping a foreign intelligence service, and Denmark expelled Russia's defense attaché. The target, rapper Kyivstoner, is a US citizen — and Denmark is a NATO ally.

Full brief

Danish police and the Danish Security and Intelligence Service (PET) announced on Friday, November 20, 2026, that they had foiled an attempt by Russian intelligence services to assassinate the US-Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner ahead of a concert north of Copenhagen on September 25. Police arrested three men with links to the Danish criminal underworld shortly before the concert; two Danish men appeared in court on Friday charged under Section 108 of Denmark's Criminal Code — assisting a foreign intelligence service — as well as attempted murder. Both denied wrongdoing. Prosecutors said the men were driving toward the venue with two loaded pistols and two knives when police stopped them at about 9:55 p.m., five minutes before the concert was due to begin; the concert was cancelled. PET head Rasmus Kieffer-Kristensen said: 'It is our clear assessment that the assassination attempt was planned by the Russian intelligence services.' Denmark expelled Russia's defense attaché, and Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen called the alleged plot 'an unacceptable transgression.' Russia denied involvement; its embassy in Copenhagen said the accusations were not backed with proof. On Saturday, November 21, Denmark urged fellow EU members to tighten travel curbs on Russian citizens, and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen condemned the attempt. PET also said in September that Russia was recruiting Danish citizens to help plan sabotage against Danish defense firms with ties to Ukraine.

⚠ The target is a US citizen, and the plot — if Denmark's attribution is right — is a Russian intelligence operation on NATO soil. It shows the hybrid campaign reaching into allied countries and targeting Americans, not just Ukrainians.
Confidence

High that Denmark announced the attribution, the arrests, the charges and the expulsion — PET statement, court documents, CNN and Reuters. The Russian-intelligence planning itself is Denmark's assessment, attributed and denied by Moscow, not independently confirmed.

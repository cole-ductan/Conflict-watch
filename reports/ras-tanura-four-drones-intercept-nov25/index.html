Drones Downed at Ras Tanura

Yemen's Houthis say they attacked Saudi oil refinery and Riyadh financial district

lowVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi drones — Saudi Arabia · Tuesday, November 24, 2026 at 4:48 PM CT

In plain English

Saudi air defences shot down four drones over Ras Tanura, home to the kingdom's largest refinery and a giant oil export terminal. Debris fell but nobody was hurt. The Houthis claimed they hit the refinery — the coalition did not confirm that.

Full brief

What happened

The Saudi-led coalition said air defences intercepted and destroyed four drones over Ras Tanura in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province on Wednesday morning; coalition spokesman Turki al-Malki said fragments fell from the interceptions with no injuries in an initial assessment (Reuters; OilPrice). Contested / unverified: the Houthis claimed the strike on the Ras Tanura refinery was "accurate and direct" and caused fires — unverified and contested: the coalition did not say the refinery had been struck. The Houthis also claimed a separate attack on Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District — unverified, no confirmation, and distinct from the Nov 24 PIF Tower attack already on this dashboard.

Why this matters for the US

Ras Tanura is one of the world's largest crude export terminals, and a successful hit there would jolt oil markets — Brent rose over 2% on the claim — and threaten the energy flows US forces in the Gulf help protect.

⚠ The Houthi claims — a direct hit on the Ras Tanura refinery causing fires, and a separate attack on Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District — are unverified; the coalition confirmed only the interceptions, with debris and no injuries.
Confidence

High on the intercept (coalition statement via Reuters); Low on the Houthi refinery-hit and Riyadh claims.

Second Tanker Hit Monday

UKMTO Warning 208-26: tanker attacked 7 nautical miles north of Khasab, engine-room fire

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime attack — Strait of Hormuz · Monday, November 16, 2026 at 1:00 PM CT

In plain English

A tanker crossing the Strait of Hormuz was attacked on Monday afternoon, about 7 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman — the narrowest part of the strait. The attack disabled the vessel and set its engine room on fire; the crew are all safe and no one was hurt. It is the second confirmed tanker strike of the day, after a tanker was hit early Monday morning. The British maritime agency that reported it did not name the ship or say who attacked it. Iran's navy claims this was the fourth tanker it has hit in 24 hours, but that link has not been confirmed. A maritime tracker reports a distress alert from a tanker called the Tara Gas in the same area at about the same time, but that has not been confirmed as the same vessel.

Full brief

What happened

UKMTO Warning 208-26: UKMTO has received a third-party report of an attack on a tanker transiting the Strait of Hormuz, 7 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, at 19:00 UTC on Monday, November 16. The attack disabled the vessel and caused a fire in the engine room. The crew have been reported safe; no casualties. Damage assessment and environmental impact are unknown at time of report. Vessel — UKMTO did not name the vessel, its flag, cargo or destination. Maritime tracker VesselTracker reports a Digital Selective Calling distress alert broadcast by the tanker Tara Gas at 19:15 UTC from 26°22′N 056°18′E, about 10–12 nautical miles northwest of Khasab — the same time and area as the UKMTO report — but UKMTO has not confirmed the Tara Gas is the vessel in Warning 208-26. Attacker — not identified. No claim of responsibility appears in the UKMTO reporting. Contested attribution — Iran's navy says it has now hit four oil tankers in the past 24 hours and its announcement appears to relate to this incident, but that link has not been independently confirmed, and UKMTO did not say who carried out the attack. This is separate from the Iranian Army Navy's claim that the morning tanker fire (On Phoenix, Warning 207-26) was caused by a naval mine, and separate from the IRGC's wider mine claims carried in the Unverified section.

Context

the second UKMTO-verified tanker attack on November 16, after Warning 207-26 (the On Phoenix, struck by an unknown projectile at 05:58 UTC, engine-room fire, crew safe) and the third in two days after Warning 205-26 on Sunday (the VLCC Al Dhannah, Arabian Gulf). The Ambrey report of a merchant vessel struck about 20 nautical miles northeast of Khasab remains ambiguous and is not this incident — different position and no UKMTO warning.

Why this matters for the US

the Strait of Hormuz carries a large share of global oil and LNG shipments; a second verified strike in a single day, in the strait's narrowest stretch, shows the shipping campaign is continuing and raises war-risk costs for American energy and commercial traffic.

⚠ Attacker unidentified — vessel unnamed by UKMTO; Iran's claim of a fourth strike in 24 hours is attributed and unconfirmed
Confidence

High for the UKMTO facts (verified against the UKMTO Warning 208-26 PDF); Medium for the Tara Gas identity (single tracker report of a distress alert, not confirmed by UKMTO).

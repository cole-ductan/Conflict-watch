Southern Drone Intercepted

Coalition: UAV Launched by Terrorist Houthi Militia toward Southern Region Intercepted, Destroyed

lowVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi drones — Saudi Arabia · Tuesday, November 24, 2026 at 4:55 PM CT

In plain English

Saudi air defences shot down a Houthi drone heading for the kingdom's southern region on Tuesday, the Saudi-led coalition said. No damage or casualties were reported. It was the latest in a near-daily run of Houthi drone and missile launches at Saudi Arabia.

Full brief

What happened

The Saudi-led coalition said on Tuesday, Nov 24, that its air defences intercepted and destroyed a Houthi drone launched toward Saudi Arabia's southern region, near the Yemeni border, the Saudi Press Agency reported, citing coalition spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki al-Malki. No damage or casualties were reported, and the coalition did not say what the drone was aimed at.

Context

the intercept comes on a day of rising Saudi-Houthi escalation: hours later the coalition issued an evacuation warning for Yemen's Hodeidah port, and the Houthis threatened Saudi Red Sea ports. Coalition air defences have intercepted Houthi drones and missiles over Saudi Arabia almost daily since the renewed exchange began in mid-November.

Contested claims

none: the intercept is the coalition's own statement, carried by the official Saudi Press Agency; the Houthis have not commented on this launch.

Why this matters for the US

the Houthis keep probing Saudi air defences with drones, and every launch tests the systems that also protect US personnel and facilities in the kingdom — but this one was stopped with no damage.

⚠ No damage or casualties reported; the drone's intended target was not stated.
Confidence

High that the intercept happened as described — the Saudi Press Agency's own Nov 24 report, citing the coalition spokesman's statement. No independent confirmation of the drone's target, and no Houthi comment.

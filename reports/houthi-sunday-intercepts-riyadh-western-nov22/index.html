Houthi Attacks Intercepted

Coalition: Air Defenses Intercept, Destroy Ballistic Missile Launched by Terrorist Houthi Militia toward Western Region

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi attacks — Saudi Arabia · Sunday, November 22, 2026 at 4:29 PM CT

In plain English

Saudi defences intercepted a Houthi drone headed for Riyadh and a ballistic missile aimed at the kingdom's western region on Sunday, the Saudi-led coalition said. No damage or casualties were reported. It was the latest round of Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia in the current escalation.

Full brief

The Saudi-led coalition said on Sunday, November 22, 2026, that its air defences intercepted and destroyed a Houthi ballistic missile launched toward the western region of Saudi Arabia, coalition spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Malki said in a statement carried by the Saudi Press Agency. The coalition also said it intercepted and destroyed a Houthi drone launched toward the Riyadh region the same day. Saudi civil defence issued emergency alerts in Riyadh, Abha and Khamis Mushait before lifting them minutes later. No damage or casualties were reported from either interception. The Houthis, an Iran-aligned movement, have fired missiles and drones at Saudi targets repeatedly since the regional escalation began; the US Embassy in Riyadh issued a nationwide security alert on Saturday urging Americans in the kingdom to exercise heightened vigilance, citing the increased threat of Houthi attacks. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree separately claimed the group attacked King Khalid International Airport and Saudi oil facilities on Saturday — that claim is not independently confirmed.

⚠ Houthi attacks on Riyadh and Saudi energy infrastructure threaten Americans living and working in the kingdom — the US Embassy issued a nationwide alert on Saturday — and a key US partner's oil infrastructure, in the same Iran-linked escalation hitting Gulf states.
Confidence

High that the coalition reported both interceptions — the western-region missile interception is confirmed by the Saudi Press Agency's official statement. The Houthi claims about Saturday's airport and oil-facility attacks are unverified.

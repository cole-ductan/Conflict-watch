Putin meets US envoys

Putin meets US envoys Witkoff and Kushner; Starshield over Ukraine reportedly limited

mediumVerifiedRussia–Ukraine · Diplomacy / Battlefield comms · Thursday, November 12, 2026 at 9:00 AM CT

In plain English

President Putin met Trump's envoys in Moscow and agreed in principle to let Europeans join the next Ukraine peace talks. Separately, Ukraine says its military satellite internet over the front line is being slowed — Kyiv suspects US pressure, but that is unconfirmed.

Full brief

President Vladimir Putin met US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner in Moscow on Nov 12 and agreed in principle to let European representatives join the next round of Ukraine peace talks, Witkoff said after the meeting via Reuters. Putin warned that sending Western troops into Ukraine after a peace deal would trigger a resumption of the war. Separately, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Nov 12 that Starshield (military Starlink) service over Ukraine was being systematically limited to 161 km/h, affecting the whole front line; Kyiv officials told the Financial Times they suspect deliberate US pressure in the peace talks; the US offered no explanation.

⚠ Starshield 'US pressure' is Kyiv's suspicion — unconfirmed
Confidence

High that the Moscow meeting and the European-representatives agreement were reported by Reuters; the Starshield limitation is Zelenskyy's statement, and the 'deliberate US pressure' reading is Kyiv officials' suspicion as reported to the FT — not a US-confirmed fact.

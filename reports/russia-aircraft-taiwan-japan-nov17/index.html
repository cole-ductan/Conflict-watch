Russian Jets Near Taiwan

Taiwan, Japan flag incursions by Russian aircraft as Putin set to arrive in China (Reuters, November 17, 2026)

low-mediumVerifiedChina–Taiwan · Airspace violation — Japan / Taiwan · Monday, November 16, 2026 at 10:33 PM CT

In plain English

Two Russian military aircraft flew off Taiwan's east coast on Tuesday, Taiwan's defence ministry said. The same day, Japan said two aircraft believed to be Russian entered its airspace off Okinoshima island in southwestern Japan, and Japanese fighter jets were scrambled in response. Japan lodged a strong protest with Moscow. No shots were fired and no damage was reported.

Full brief

What happened

Taiwan's defence ministry said on Tuesday, November 17, that it detected two Russian military aircraft flying off the island's east coast; after flying through airspace to the island's east, the aircraft exited Taiwan's air defence identification zone south of Taitung county. Earlier the same day, Japan lodged a strong protest with Moscow after two aircraft believed to be Russian violated Japanese airspace off Okinoshima island in southwestern Fukuoka; Chief Cabinet Secretary Minoru Kihara called the violation “a serious infringement of Japan's sovereignty” and “completely unacceptable.” Japan's Self-Defense Forces scrambled fighter aircraft and issued warnings. Japan's defence ministry said the suspected Russian aircraft subsequently transited the Tsushima Strait, passed between Yonaguni Island and Taiwan, and proceeded into the Pacific Ocean.

Context

the reports come ahead of Russian President Vladimir Putin's arrival in Shenzhen for this week's APEC summit. Japan's ministry said the last confirmed violation of its airspace by Russian aircraft was off Rebun Island near Hokkaido in September 2024. In 2023 Taiwan spotted two Russian warships off its eastern coast. Contested / unconfirmed — neither Taiwan nor Japan identified the aircraft type. Reuters reports the Taiwan and Japan sightings were “probably about the same aircraft”; a map issued by Japan's defence ministry shows a flight path to Taiwan's east heading south, implying the same pair, but this is not confirmed.

Why this matters for the US

Japan is a US treaty ally — a confirmed Russian military airspace violation of Japan is a direct challenge to an ally the US is bound to defend, and Russian military aircraft operating between Yonaguni and Taiwan put pressure on the same waters US forces patrol. It also signals closer Russia–China military coordination days before Putin meets Xi at APEC.

⚠ Aircraft type unidentified; Taiwan and Japan sightings probably the same aircraft, not confirmed
Confidence

High that Taiwan detected two Russian military aircraft off its east coast and that Japan protested an airspace violation (Reuters reporting; Taiwan defence ministry statement; Japan defence ministry and Chief Cabinet Secretary Minoru Kihara on the record). Not confirmed whether the aircraft Taiwan spotted and the aircraft Japan tracked were the same pair — Reuters says they were “probably” the same aircraft; Japan's defence ministry map implies it but does not confirm it. Aircraft type not identified by either ministry.

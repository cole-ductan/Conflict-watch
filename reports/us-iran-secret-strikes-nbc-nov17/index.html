Secret Strikes Revealed

U.S. and Iran trade attacks almost daily that the Pentagon doesn't always acknowledge (NBC News, Nov 17, 2026)

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Unacknowledged strikes — US / Iran · Tuesday, November 17, 2026 at 8:08 PM CT

In plain English

The US and Iran have actually been trading strikes almost every day, and the Pentagon has not publicly acknowledged most of the American strikes. Two US officials told NBC News that US forces have repeatedly hit Iranian missile launchers and military sites over the past two months, while Iran has hit US military sites nearly every day in return. The report also reveals that an Iranian missile hit a ship in the Strait of Hormuz on September 14 and injured eight US Marines — something the Pentagon never announced.

Full brief

What happened

NBC News reported on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, citing two US officials and a person familiar with the operations, that American forces have repeatedly targeted Iranian missile launchers and military installations over the past two months, and that Iran has responded with attacks on US military facilities, naval assets, and commercial shipping in the Persian Gulf. The officials said the Pentagon has not publicly acknowledged most US strikes, and that US forces often target missile launchers and other systems Iran uses to attack vessels and Gulf states. The report also disclosed, citing three US officials, that on September 14 an Iranian anti-ship cruise missile struck a vessel operating in the Strait of Hormuz, injuring eight US Marines — smoke inhalation and possible traumatic brain injuries; all returned to duty — and the Pentagon never publicly announced that attack either. The latest operation named in the report is the November 17 US strike on an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island, already covered on this dashboard as “US Strikes Qeshm Again.” The Pentagon declined to comment on the NBC report; the White House did not respond. Contested/attributed — the strike pattern and the September 14 incident are anonymous-official claims reported by NBC, not Pentagon-confirmed facts; the Institute for the Study of War's November 18 update independently summarises the same NBC account.

Why this matters for the US

The war the public has been told is “effectively over” is actually a near-daily exchange — US forces are quietly striking Iranian launchers while Iran hits US sites almost every day — and eight Marines were already hurt in September without the Pentagon ever saying so. The real tempo of the war is much higher than the official record shows.

Microsoft 365 outage restored

Microsoft 365 outage takes down Outlook, Teams and Copilot worldwide — Microsoft says network fault, not a cyberattack

lowVerifiedCyber / Homeland · Cyber / Cloud outage · Friday, November 13, 2026 at 10:17 PM CT

In plain English

Microsoft's email and workplace apps went down worldwide on Nov 12–13, hitting Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and Copilot in at least 74 countries. Microsoft says it was a network fault from an earlier upgrade, not a cyberattack, and service was fully restored on Nov 14.

Full brief

The outage began around 1 p.m. ET on Thursday, Nov 12, with Exchange Online mailboxes failing in the Americas before widening to users in any region (incidents EX1550543 / MO1550821). Downdetector logged more than 120,000 Outlook problem reports at the peak, from 74 countries. Microsoft said servers lost capacity after a core process became overwhelmed, began reversing a recent network configuration change, and said there is no indication of malicious activity; executive Charles Lamanna ruled out a cyberattack. Its final update, at 04:17 UTC on Nov 14 (11:17 p.m. ET, Nov 13), said services across the previously affected environments had fully recovered and the email backlog had been processed, with impact ending at 4:10 p.m. UTC on Nov 13. Microsoft's preliminary cause: an issue during an earlier network capacity upgrade that surfaced following a recent network change. A full post-incident report has not yet been published. No connection to the Nov 13 FAA nationwide ground stop has been established.

⚠ Resolved — Microsoft says network fault, not a cyberattack; full report still pending
Confidence

High that the outage happened, that Microsoft restored service, and that Microsoft says it was not a cyberattack (Microsoft 365 status / Service health feed, corroborated by independent reporting). The full root-cause report is still pending.

Riyadh Tower Attacked

Saudi-led coalition says tower in Riyadh finance district targeted by 'terrorist attack'

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi attacks — Saudi Arabia · Tuesday, November 24, 2026 at 9:20 AM CT

In plain English

A tower in Riyadh's financial district was targeted in an attack today. Saudi civil defence says 11 people were injured. The Saudi-led coalition calls it a 'terrorist attack' but hasn't named who did it. Regional reporting attributes it to the Houthis. Reuters confirms the attack happened, not who is responsible.

Full brief

What's changed: On Tuesday the Saudi-led coalition said a tower in Riyadh's financial district was targeted in a "terrorist attack." Saudi civil defence reported 11 people injured. The coalition gave no further details and did not name an attacker. Regional reporting (The National) attributes the attack to the Houthis; Reuters confirms the attack occurred but does not establish responsibility. Evidence and confidence: High that the attack occurred and 11 were injured (coalition statement plus civil defence, via Reuters and Roya News). Moderate, provisional on Houthi responsibility (regional attribution plus the Houthis' ongoing missile and drone campaign against Riyadh, including the Nov 19 PIF Tower strike and the Nov 8 Aramco building hit; the coalition has not named the Houthis for this incident). Direct Iranian involvement is unverified. Additional developments: This is the third financial-district landmark hit in under three weeks — the Aramco building on Nov 8 and the PIF Tower, Riyadh's tallest skyscraper and headquarters of the Saudi sovereign wealth fund, on Nov 19, which suffered material damage but no injuries. The Houthis have been firing missiles and drones at Riyadh throughout November, and the coalition has intercepted several. US implications:

Why this matters for the US

Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia's financial center threaten a key US partner and Gulf stability. The Houthis are an Iranian proxy in the active conflict, and attacks on Riyadh raise the risk of escalation that could pull in US forces and assets in the region.

⚠ Third financial-district landmark hit in under three weeks, after the Aramco building on Nov 8 and the PIF Tower on Nov 19. The coalition has not named an attacker — Houthi responsibility is regionally attributed and provisional, and direct Iranian involvement is unverified.

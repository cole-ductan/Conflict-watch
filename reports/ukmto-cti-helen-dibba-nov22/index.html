Bulker Hit Off UAE

Bulk carrier CTI Helen struck by projectile off UAE coast, fire reported

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime incident — Gulf of Oman · Sunday, November 22, 2026 at 4:26 AM CT

In plain English

A bulk carrier called the CTI Helen was hit by an unknown projectile while anchored off the United Arab Emirates coast, near Khor Fakkan, and caught fire. The strike happened on Sunday morning, but only came to light on Tuesday, when UK maritime authorities received a late report of it. No casualties have been reported.

Full brief

What happened

The Hong Kong-managed bulk carrier CTI Helen (50,300 deadweight tonnes, built 2001) was struck on its port side by an unknown projectile while anchored at Dibba outer port limit anchorage, about 23 nautical miles northeast of Khor Fakkan in the United Arab Emirates, at 10:26 UTC on Sunday, November 22, 2026; a fire was reported on board and no casualties were reported. The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations centre (UKMTO) published the incident on Tuesday, November 24, as a time-late report (Ship & Bunker, Nov 24; TASS, Nov 24); shipping journal Tradewinds, citing maritime intelligence firm Vanguard Tech, names the vessel and dates the strike to November 22.

Context

The incident surfaced publicly on Tuesday morning with several outlets carrying the same details; one Reuters wire described the strike as happening on Monday, but UKMTO's own statement and TASS both place it on Sunday, November 22, and that is the date used here. It is a separate incident from the November 22 Gulf of Oman advisory (217-26) about a tanker and military forces off Chabahar, and from the three tankers struck on Monday, November 23.

Why this matters for the US

the strike extends the pattern of attacks on commercial shipping at the Strait of Hormuz's eastern approaches — the same waters US-escorted traffic must transit.

⚠ No casualties reported; UKMTO has not issued a numbered warning for this incident yet, and one wire report dates it Monday rather than Sunday.
Confidence

High that a bulk carrier was struck on its port side by an unknown projectile at Dibba anchorage with a fire on board and no casualties — UKMTO's own statement, quoted by Ship & Bunker and TASS. High on the Sunday, November 22 date and 10:26 UTC time — UKMTO's statement via Ship & Bunker and TASS; one Reuters wire says Monday, which conflicts with UKMTO's own account. Medium that the vessel is the CTI Helen — the name and date come from Tradewinds citing Vanguard Tech, not from UKMTO, which did not name the vessel.

Missiles Cross Into Poland

Russian missiles and drone entered Polish airspace during overnight Kyiv barrage, Polish defence minister says (Reuters, Nov 20, 2026)

mediumVerifiedRussia–Ukraine · NATO airspace — Poland · Thursday, November 19, 2026 at 6:41 PM CT

In plain English

During Russia's huge overnight missile-and-drone attack on Ukraine, three objects — likely two missiles and a drone — crossed into Poland's airspace, Poland's defence minister said, calling it a Russian 'provocation.' Poland is a NATO ally of the United States. No damage or casualties in Poland were reported. The barrage was one of the largest of the war: more than 110 missiles and about 400 drones, hitting three bridges across Kyiv's Dnipro River and energy infrastructure across Ukraine.

Full brief

What happened

During Russia's overnight attack on Ukraine into Friday, Nov 20, Russia violated Poland's airspace with three objects that were likely two missiles and a drone, the Polish defence minister said, denouncing it as a Russian 'provocation' (Reuters, Nov 20).

Context

The incursion came during one of the largest Russian air attacks of the war: President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia fired more than 110 missiles, 27 of them ballistic, and about 400 drones, including 120 jet-powered drones, over more than ten hours, targeting energy, logistics and railway infrastructure across Ukraine. Three bridges across the Dnipro River in Kyiv — Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi — were hit, the biggest strikes on the capital's river crossings of the war. Eight people were killed and 28 injured, Ukrainian authorities said; a water utility facility in Kyiv was hit, killing three employees. Russia said it launched a 'massive strike' on military and energy facilities. The attack was retaliation for Ukraine's third strike this week on the Kerch Bridge to Crimea, and came hours after Presidents Putin, Trump and Xi met in Shenzhen.

Why this matters for the US

Poland is a NATO treaty ally hosting US forces. Russian weapons entering allied airspace during a strike is the kind of spillover that can pull the alliance in — even without an attack on Polish soil, it tests NATO's response and the US air-defence commitments in Poland.

⚠ Object types are the Polish minister's assessment ('likely'), not confirmed; no damage or casualties in Poland reported; Russia has not commented on the incursion in the wire.

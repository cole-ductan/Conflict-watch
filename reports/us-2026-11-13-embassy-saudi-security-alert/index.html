Embassy alert: Saudi Arabia

US Mission to Saudi Arabia issues countrywide Security Alert: Americans urged to exercise heightened vigilance amid increased Houthi attack threat and Iranian efforts to target US government officials; extreme caution at Saudi airports

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Security Alert / Saudi Arabia · Friday, November 13, 2026 at 4:00 PM CT

In plain English

The US Embassy in Saudi Arabia has told Americans there to be extra careful. It says the Houthis have been attacking Saudi airports and infrastructure, and US government staff now need special permission to use any airport in the country.

Full brief

The US Mission to Saudi Arabia issued a countrywide Security Alert dated November 13 warning that Americans in the Middle East should exercise heightened vigilance “due to the increased threat of armed attacks by Iranian-supported Houthis and Iranian efforts to target U.S. government officials in the region.” The alert notes Houthi attacks on Saudi civilian airports and critical infrastructure have caused civilian casualties and severe travel disruptions, warns of potential attacks, airspace closures, flight cancellations and travel disruptions, and says US government employees now require Chief of Mission authorization to use any airport in Saudi Arabia. It restates the October 23 ordered departure of non-emergency US government employees and family members. The State Department Travel Advisory level is unchanged (Level 3, “Reconsider Travel”). The alert followed the Houthis' November 13 claims of drone strikes on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport and an East-West Pipeline pumping station, and their ballistic-missile claims against Saudi forces in Jazan.

⚠ Embassy assessment — underlying Houthi claims remain unverified
Confidence

High — the original source is the US Embassy site itself. The threat warning is the embassy's own assessment, not an independently verified incident; the underlying Houthi claims remain unverified.

FBI Database Breach

FBI's criminal information database hacked, CNN reports

mediumVerifiedCyber / Homeland · Cyber / FBI · Friday, November 20, 2026 at 9:30 PM CT

In plain English

The FBI is investigating a suspected break-in at the National Crime Information Center — the nationwide database police across the country use to check warrants, criminal histories, and background checks. CNN reported it Friday, citing two people briefed on the investigation. No one has said who did it or what, if anything, was taken.

Full brief

The FBI is investigating a suspected breach of the National Crime Information Center (NCIC) database, CNN reported Friday, citing two people briefed on the investigation. NCIC is the nationwide system federal, state, and local law enforcement use to share criminal-justice information, including warrants, criminal histories, and background checks. The FBI did not respond to Reuters' request for comment. It would be the second intrusion involving an FBI system in recent months, after the September breach by the digital extortion group ShinyHunters of an FBI jobs website that compromised data on current and former FBI employees. It is the latest in a string of cyber incidents that have alarmed US officials over the past week, which also included a global outage affecting hundreds of millions of Microsoft users and a nationwide halt to air travel that disrupted more than 18,000 flights. Contested / unverified: whether a breach actually occurred (described only as “suspected” / “apparent”), who is responsible (no attribution has been made), and whether any data was taken — none of that has been confirmed.

Why this matters for the US

The National Crime Information Center is the backbone database US law enforcement relies on every day; a compromise could expose sensitive criminal-justice data and shake trust in a core federal system.

⚠ Suspected breach only — whether it occurred, who is responsible, and whether any data was taken are all unconfirmed; the FBI did not respond to Reuters' request for comment.

Missiles downed over Riyadh

Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted two Houthi ballistic missiles toward Riyadh and three drones in the Eastern Province, Sunday Nov 15

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Missiles / Interceptions — Saudi Arabia · Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 9:12 PM CT

In plain English

Saudi Arabia says its air defenses stopped two ballistic missiles aimed at the capital, Riyadh, and three drones over the oil-rich Eastern Province. Nobody was hurt and nothing was damaged, according to the coalition. The Houthis have been stepping up attacks on Saudi Arabia in recent days.

Full brief

The Saudi-led coalition said on Sunday it intercepted two Houthi ballistic missiles launched toward the Saudi capital Riyadh and three drones in the kingdom's oil-rich Eastern Province. Coalition spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki al-Maliki confirmed the interceptions in a statement published on X. No casualties or damage were reported.

Context

the Houthis have intensified attacks on the Saudi capital in recent days — a Houthi attack last week damaged an administrative building of state oil company Saudi Aramco in Riyadh's financial hub, and the Eastern Province, which holds most of the kingdom's oil and gas reserves, has been subject to multiple Houthi attacks in recent weeks. This is distinct from the coalition's Saturday Nov 14 interceptions (two drones, Riyadh and the southern region) already on the dashboard, and does NOT confirm the Houthis' separate unverified Nov 13 claims about Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport or an East-West Pipeline pumping station.

⚠ Coalition account only — no independent verification of wreckage or damage
Confidence

High that the coalition made this announcement — Reuters original reporting the coalition statement and the spokesperson's X post. The interception itself is the coalition's account — no independent verification of wreckage or damage published.

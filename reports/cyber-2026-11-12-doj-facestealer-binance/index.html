Crypto theft ring busted

DOJ and FBI disrupt North Korean 'FaceStealer' crypto-theft scheme; $1.2 billion Binance seizure

lowVerifiedCyber / Homeland · Cyber / Crypto theft · Thursday, November 12, 2026 at 8:00 AM CT

In plain English

US agents shut down a North Korean scheme that used fake video calls to steal more than $400 million in crypto, and separately froze $1.2 billion stolen from the Binance exchange. The frozen money has not been recovered or handed back yet — that process is ongoing.

Full brief

The US Department of Justice announced coordinated actions against North Korean government schemes to fund the regime through cryptocurrency theft: (1) seizure of cloud infrastructure behind “FaceStealer,” a social-engineering scheme the FBI says stole more than $400 million in crypto over about 18 months using hijacked accounts and fake Zoom/Teams video calls playing secretly recorded webcam footage and deepfakes of known contacts; the FBI seized more than 900 victim webcam videos under a federal warrant and published FLASH alert AC-111226-MS; (2) court-authorized seizure and restraint of more than $1.2 billion in crypto stolen from Binance on or about Oct 29, 2026 — part of $2.7 billion-plus frozen so far — attributed by the FBI to North Korean military hackers it calls TraderTraitor, who stole $6 billion-plus. The two actions are separate; FaceStealer did NOT cause the Binance theft. The $1.2 billion is a court-authorized seizure and restraint within an ongoing freeze, with forfeiture proceedings ongoing — it is not presented here as recovered or forfeited.

⚠ $1.2bn is seizure/restraint in an ongoing freeze — not recovered or forfeited

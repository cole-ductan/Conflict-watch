Riyadh Missiles Intercepted

Saudi Arabia says it intercepts three Houthi missiles targeting Riyadh (Reuters, Nov 20, 2026)

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi missiles — Saudi Arabia · Friday, November 20, 2026 at 5:09 PM CT

In plain English

On Friday evening, Saudi Arabia issued two air-threat alerts in its capital, Riyadh — the first such alerts since a Houthi missile struck the city's tallest skyscraper on Thursday. A Reuters witness saw a projectile streak across the sky and heard a loud bang, and the Saudi-led coalition said its air defenses intercepted and destroyed three Houthi ballistic missiles fired toward the city. Civil defense later said the danger had passed; no casualties or damage were reported.

Full brief

What happened

Saudi Civil Defense issued two air-threat alerts in Riyadh on Friday evening, Nov 20, the first at 23:09 UTC (5:09 PM CT) — the first such alerts since a Houthi missile struck the city's tallest skyscraper on Thursday. A Reuters witness in the capital saw a projectile streak across the sky and heard a huge bang. The Saudi-led coalition said in a statement at 23:40 UTC (5:40 PM CT), reported by Reuters, that its air defenses intercepted and destroyed three ballistic missiles fired by the Houthis toward Riyadh. Civil Defense later said the danger had passed, and no casualties or damage were reported.

Context

this is the second Houthi attack on the Saudi capital in two days, following Thursday's strike on Riyadh's tallest skyscraper, and it came hours after the coalition struck the Houthi-held port of Hodeidah in Yemen on Friday — both already on this dashboard.

Contested claims

the exact number of missiles (three) and the interception outcome are the coalition's own account, not independently verified. The Houthis were reported on X as saying they were attacking targets across Saudi Arabia including Riyadh, but the Houthi military spokesman's Telegram channel carried no claim as of a late check.

Why this matters for the US

Riyadh hosts the US Embassy and American personnel, and this is the second Houthi attack on the Saudi capital in two days — the Houthi campaign against a key US ally is escalating the day after the coalition struck Hodeidah port, raising the risk to US people and interests in the kingdom.

⚠ Missile count (three) and interception outcome are the coalition's account, not independently verified; Houthi claim reported on X only — nothing on the military spokesman's Telegram channel; no casualties or damage reported.

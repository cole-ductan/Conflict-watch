G7 warns over Taiwan

G7: China's activities east of Taiwan 'threaten regional stability' — strongest unified statement to date

mediumVerifiedChina–Taiwan · G7 declaration / A2/AD · Wednesday, November 11, 2026 at 3:00 PM CT

In plain English

The G7 — the group of seven major democracies — issued its strongest joint warning yet about China's military activity east of Taiwan, saying it threatens shipping and regional stability. China has been running exercises designed to block outside forces from helping Taiwan in a crisis.

Full brief

The G7 foreign ministers' joint declaration, published late Nov 11 in Paris, said China's increasingly assertive activities east of Taiwan 'threaten regional stability, freedom of navigation and the safety of international shipping' — the strongest unified G7 statement on this to date.

Context

Taiwan Defence Minister Wellington Koo said Nov 10 that China is conducting anti-access/area-denial (A2/AD) exercises in the Western Pacific with the Liaoning carrier group, aimed at blocking outside (US/allied) forces from intervening in a Taiwan crisis. On Nov 12, Taiwan's defence ministry detected a 29-aircraft J-16-led joint combat-readiness patrol around Taiwan, and Xi Jinping said reunification would 'certainly' be achieved.

⚠ Pressure campaign + formal G7 warning — no attack
Confidence

High that the G7 declaration was published as quoted; the A2/AD characterization is Taiwan's defence minister's assessment, attributed as such. No invasion or blockade was declared on Nov 11–12.

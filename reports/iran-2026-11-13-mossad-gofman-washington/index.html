Israel presses for strikes

Mossad director Roman Gofman in Washington as Netanyahu presses Trump to resume Iran war

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Diplomacy / Israel–US · Friday, November 13, 2026 at 8:00 AM CT

In plain English

Israel's spy chief is in Washington while Prime Minister Netanyahu pushes President Trump to restart the war on Iran. So far Trump has said no, preferring the naval blockade and economic pressure. No new strike has been decided.

Full brief

Axios reported that Mossad director Roman Gofman — one of Netanyahu's closest confidants, in the role since June — is in Washington on Friday for talks with senior Trump administration officials about the Iran war, meeting CIA Director John Ratcliffe and expected to travel to Miami to meet White House envoy Steve Witkoff. Axios, citing three sources, says Netanyahu is pressing Trump to resume military operations against Iran; a US official says Trump has so far turned that down, backing the naval blockade and economic pressure approach favoured by Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the UAE. The reporting is corroborated by CNN and The Jerusalem Post. CNN adds that Netanyahu has even considered a unilateral Israeli strike on Iran, which would still need Trump's approval. No decision to resume strikes has been announced and there is no new strike.

⚠ No new strike — no decision to resume strikes announced

High — Axios via three sources, corroborated by CNN and The Jerusalem Post.

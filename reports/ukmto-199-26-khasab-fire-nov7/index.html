Tanker Burns off Khasab

UKMTO Warning 199-26: tanker on fire northwest of Khasab, Oman; crew evacuated

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime attack — Strait of Hormuz · Saturday, November 7, 2026 at 1:20 PM CT

In plain English

A tanker caught fire off Oman on Saturday evening, Nov 7, and its crew had to abandon ship — the first crew evacuation in this run of tanker attacks. No injuries were reported. Open-source analysts have tentatively identified the ship as the product tanker Navig8 Messi, linked to the UAE's ADNOC shipping arm, but that has not been officially confirmed. No one has claimed the attack.

Full brief

What happened

UKMTO Warning 199-26: military authorities reported a tanker on fire at 19:20 UTC on Saturday, November 7, about 8 nautical miles northwest of Khasab, Oman. The crew were evacuated; no injuries reported. UKMTO initially filed it as suspicious activity and later reclassified it as an attack — the initial and reclassified notices are the same incident, not separate attacks. Vessel — not named by UKMTO. Open-source analysts tentatively identify it as the Navig8 Messi (IMO 9482859), a product tanker owned by Navig8, of which ADNOC Logistics & Services holds 80% — tentative only; neither UKMTO, JMIC nor the operator had confirmed it. Unverified footage showed a fire amidships with at least two firefighting tugs at the scene. Attacker — not identified. No claim of responsibility. The IRGC separately claimed three tankers caught fire that evening — unverified, and no source links that claim to this incident.

Context

first of two UKMTO incidents that evening (200-26 followed one hour later); the eighth and ninth ships hit in the Strait of Hormuz, Arabian Gulf and Gulf of Oman since Nov 1, per the Red Zone count.

Why this matters for the US

a tanker fire forcing a full crew evacuation in the Hormuz chokepoint directly threatens US shipping and trade and shows the tanker campaign escalating from strikes to vessel losses.

⚠ First crew evacuation of the tanker campaign — vessel identity tentative, attacker unidentified
Confidence

High for the UKMTO facts; Medium-Low for the vessel identity.

Waterline Blast off Oman

UKMTO Warning 197-26: tanker reports explosion at port-side waterline east of Khasab, Oman

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime incident — Strait of Hormuz · Thursday, November 5, 2026 at 4:00 PM CT

In plain English

A tanker leaving the Strait of Hormuz reported an explosion at its waterline off Oman on Thursday evening, Nov 5. The crew are safe and the ship kept going under its own power. No one has said what caused the blast — the British maritime agency that reported it gave no cause, and Iran's claims that tankers hit mines that night have not been independently confirmed.

Full brief

What happened

UKMTO Warning 197-26: the master of a tanker transiting outbound through the Strait of Hormuz reported an explosion at the vessel's port-side waterline at 22:00 UTC on Thursday, November 5, about 16 nautical miles east of Khasab, Oman. All crew safe and accounted for; the tanker remained under its own power and continued its voyage. UKMTO said no cause or extent of damage had been specified and an investigation was underway. Vessel — not named by UKMTO or Reuters. Maritime security firm Vanguard Tech names it as the VLCC Vieira (IMO 9234654) — unconfirmed by any official body or the owner, and not presented here as fact. Cause — not established. The IRGC said on Nov 6 that two tankers had hit mines and been halted, one in the eastern strait — that claim is unverified and no source links it to this incident; it is distinct from the mine-claim family carried in the Unverified section.

Context

second verified tanker incident in two days (after 196-26, Nov 4), the day before the Nov 7 Khasab fire and projectile strike (199-26, 200-26).

Why this matters for the US

an unexplained explosion on an outbound tanker in the world's most important oil chokepoint directly threatens US shipping and trade.

⚠ Cause not established — vessel identity unconfirmed; IRGC mine claim is unverified and not linked to this incident
Confidence

High for the UKMTO/Reuters facts; Low-Medium on cause — UKMTO gives no cause, and no source links the IRGC's Nov 6 mine claims to this incident. Vessel identity unconfirmed.

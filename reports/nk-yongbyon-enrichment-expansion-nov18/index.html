North Korea Expands Enrichment

IAEA says North Korea is expanding uranium-enrichment plant at Yongbyon (Reuters, Nov 18, 2026)

mediumVerifiedNorth Korea · Nuclear enrichment — Yongbyon · Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 7:38 AM CT

In plain English

North Korea has built an extension onto its uranium-enrichment plant at Yongbyon, the site it calls the heart of its nuclear program. The UN nuclear watchdog says construction started in mid-September and the outside of the new building was finished by late October. Enrichment is also continuing at Kangson, near Pyongyang, and the plutonium reactor at Yongbyon is probably still running.

Full brief

What happened

IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi told the agency's 35-nation Board of Governors on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, that “since mid-September 2026, a further extension to the original Yongbyon enrichment facility has been under construction with the new extension externally complete by late October 2026.” Grossi said “the ongoing expansion of the DPRK's uranium enrichment programme remains a cause for serious concern.” The IAEA also reports enrichment continuing at Yongbyon and at Kangson; a second enrichment building at Yongbyon identified earlier this year could hold up to 28 centrifuge cascades; the 5-megawatt reactor that produces plutonium probably continues to operate in its seventh irradiation cycle; and a light-water reactor at Yongbyon has been operating since April 2026. Contested/attributed — the IAEA has had no access to North Korea since Pyongyang expelled its inspectors in 2009, so all of this is assessed from satellite imagery and open sources, not on-site inspection; “externally complete” means the building shell is finished — it does not confirm centrifuges are installed or running inside. North Korea has not commented on the IAEA statement.

Why this matters for the US

More enrichment capacity means North Korea can make more weapons-grade uranium for more nuclear weapons, directly threatening US treaty allies South Korea and Japan and the US troops stationed there — and it comes as President Trump is in Asia saying he wants to meet Kim Jong Un.

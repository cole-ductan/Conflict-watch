Houthi Missiles at Riyadh

Saudi air defences destroy two Houthi cruise missiles fired toward Riyadh (AP, Nov 18, 2026)

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Houthi missiles — Saudi Arabia · Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 1:06 AM CT

In plain English

Saudi air defences intercepted and destroyed two cruise missiles fired toward the capital, Riyadh, by the Houthis in Yemen, the Saudi-led coalition said on Wednesday, Nov 18. Alerts also sounded in three other Saudi cities; civil defence later said the danger had passed. No damage or casualties were reported.

Full brief

What happened

Saudi air defences intercepted and destroyed two cruise missiles fired toward Riyadh by the Iran-backed Houthis in Yemen, according to Maj. Gen. Turki al-Malki, spokesman for the Saudi-led coalition fighting the Houthis, the Associated Press reported on Wednesday, Nov 18. Alerts also sounded in three other Saudi cities, and civil defence authorities later said the danger had passed, with no further details.

Context

the missiles came a day after coalition forces launched a major air attack across Houthi-held areas of Yemen that the Houthis said killed at least 17 people; al-Malki said Tuesday's strikes destroyed '60 legitimate military targets' and aimed to weaken Houthi missile and drone capabilities. The strikes followed the withdrawal of Saudi-backed forces from areas around the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.

Why this matters for the US

The Houthis are opening a second front against a key US partner while Iran attacks US bases in the Gulf — cruise missiles aimed at the Saudi capital show the war is widening beyond the Strait of Hormuz, and Saudi Arabia is the linchpin of US regional air defence.

Confidence

High that two Houthi cruise missiles were fired toward Riyadh and intercepted (Saudi-led coalition spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki al-Malki, via AP; corroborated by Reuters). High that no damage was reported (no damage or casualty figures published).

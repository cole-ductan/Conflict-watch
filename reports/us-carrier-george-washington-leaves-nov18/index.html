Carrier Leaves Gulf

Carrier USS George Washington Leaves Middle East After Three Months (USNI News, Nov 18, 2026)

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · US force posture — Gulf · Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 3:31 PM CT

In plain English

The USS George Washington aircraft carrier has left the Middle East after three months on station, a US Pacific Fleet spokesperson confirmed to USNI News on Wednesday. The Japan-based carrier is now back with the 7th Fleet in the Asia-Pacific, conducting routine operations. Two US carrier strike groups remain in the region — the USS Theodore Roosevelt and the USS George H.W. Bush.

Full brief

What happened

The USS George Washington (CVN-73) left the Middle East after being on station for three months, a US Pacific Fleet spokesperson confirmed Wednesday to USNI News. The Japan-based carrier is now in the US 7th Fleet, conducting routine operations in the Asia-Pacific, according to spokesperson Cmdr. Katie Koenig. USNI News reported the departure at 21:31 UTC on Nov 18 (3:31 PM CT). It is unclear whether the carrier is heading home to Yokosuka, Japan, or will make a port call as it travels east.

Context

the George Washington arrived in the US 5th Fleet on Aug 19 with its escorts USS Robert Smalls (CG-62) and destroyer USS Shoup (DDG-86), having sailed from the western Pacific to relieve the USS Abraham Lincoln. The USS Theodore Roosevelt arrived in CENTCOM two days before this departure, briefly bringing the US carrier presence in the Middle East to three strike groups; with the George Washington gone, two remain — the Roosevelt and the USS George H.W. Bush, which has been in the Arabian Sea since April. US media reports put the remaining naval presence at two aircraft carriers, 13 destroyers, two cruisers and one littoral combat ship.

Contested claims

Iranian state outlet Press TV claims the remaining US warships have pulled back to positions more than 500 km from Iran's coast; that distance claim is unconfirmed and not corroborated by US sources. OSINT accounts describe the departure as a planned rotation toward Japan; USNI News says only that the carrier's destination is unclear.

Why this matters for the US

The US is down from three carrier strike groups to two in the Gulf while Iran is striking US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, Houthi missiles are hitting Saudi Arabia, and tankers are being hit in the Strait of Hormuz — so the Roosevelt and the Bush now carry the naval blockade and deterrence mission with one fewer flight deck on station.

⚠ Carrier's destination unconfirmed — USNI News says it is unclear whether the George Washington is heading home to Yokosuka or will make a port call. Iranian claims about US warships pulling back from Iran's coast are unconfirmed.

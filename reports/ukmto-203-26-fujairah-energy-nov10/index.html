Tanker Crew Injured

UKMTO Warning 203-26: tanker Fujairah Energy struck by unknown projectile in Arabian Gulf; multiple crew injured

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime attack — Arabian Gulf · November 10, 2026 · time not stated by the source

In plain English

A tanker sailing east through the Arabian Gulf was hit by an unknown projectile on Tuesday, Nov 10, and several crew members were hurt badly enough to be taken ashore for medical care. The rest of the crew are safe. India’s shipping minister identified the ship as the Fujairah Energy, and its manager confirmed one of its vessels was damaged that day. No one has said who fired.

Full brief

What happened

UKMTO Warning 203-26: a time-late report that on Tuesday, November 10, a tanker travelling eastbound in the Arabian Gulf was struck by an unknown projectile. Multiple crew members were injured and taken ashore for medical assistance; all remaining crew are safe and accounted for. No damage assessment was available at the time of the report. Vessel — identified by India’s shipping minister as the Fujairah Energy. Its manager, ADNOC Logistics & Services, confirmed on November 10 that one of its vessels had sustained damage following an attack in UAE territorial waters in the Arabian Gulf, without naming the ship. Owner: Haut Brion 45 Ltd; operator: VL8 Management (per maritime security firm Marisks). Attacker — not identified. No claim of responsibility; no government has publicly attributed it. Attribution is NOT established — this is not presented as an Iranian strike.

Context

one of four JMIC-documented tanker attacks on Nov 10–11 (with 201-26 Lima, 202-26 NCC Fajr and 204-26 Dominica Prosperity — the other three already on the dashboard). The only one of the four with reported injuries. Iran’s separate claims that tankers hit naval mines are unverified and are not part of this report.

Why this matters for the US

the first reported injuries among the Nov 10–11 tanker attacks, on a US shipping and trade route through the Gulf — the campaign against commercial shipping is now wounding crews, not just damaging steel.

⚠ Attribution NOT established — unknown projectile, no party has claimed it
Confidence

High that the strike occurred (UKMTO Warning 203-26; JMIC Advisory Update 113). Attribution NOT established — unknown projectile, no claim of responsibility, and no government has publicly attributed it.

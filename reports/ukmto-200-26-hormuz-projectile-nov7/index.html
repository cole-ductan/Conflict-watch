Projectile Strike in Hormuz

UKMTO Warning 200-26: outbound tanker struck by unknown projectile in Strait of Hormuz

mediumVerifiedIran / Gulf · Maritime attack — Strait of Hormuz · Saturday, November 7, 2026 at 2:20 PM CT

In plain English

About an hour after the Khasab tanker fire, a second tanker was hit by an unknown projectile while sailing out of the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday evening, Nov 7. Its crew are safe and there was no reported environmental damage. The ship was not named, and no one has said who fired.

Full brief

What happened

UKMTO Warning 200-26: a tanker company's security officer reported that an outbound tanker was struck by an unknown projectile at 20:20 UTC on Saturday, November 7, while transiting outbound through the Strait of Hormuz. All crew safe and accounted for; no environmental impact reported at the time. The precise incident position was not published. Vessel — not named by UKMTO; identity, flag, cargo, exact position and damage condition were not disclosed. Attacker — not identified. No claim of responsibility.

Context

second of two UKMTO incidents on the evening of Nov 7, one hour after the 199-26 Khasab fire. JMIC retained its SEVERE threat assessment for the Strait, with deliberate hostile action considered highly likely.

Why this matters for the US

two verified tanker attacks in one evening in the Hormuz chokepoint directly threaten US shipping and trade.

⚠ Vessel unnamed, attacker unidentified — second verified tanker attack that evening
Confidence

High for the UKMTO facts; vessel unnamed, attacker unidentified.

Reality, not doomsday

You do not need to go all-out doomsday prepping. Most households — especially in the city — just need enough to get through the initial stage, then a plan for what comes next.

Stage 1 — Survive72 hours to 2 weeks. Water, food, meds, cash, comms, and a warm room.
Stage 2 — StabilizeDay 3–7. Assess what is actually happening, resupply if you can, consolidate or relocate.
Stage 3 — Long-termOnly if it persists. Bulk stores, skills, and a longer-term setup — not the starting point.

This page takes no side in any conflict it covers — politically or geographically. The rules below are the whole method, and they apply to every theater, every government, and every claim.

Our mission: Reality, not doomsday.

One inclusion test

A report is published only if it threatens the US, US bases or personnel, a US treaty ally, US shipping or trade routes, or US infrastructure or cyber. Anything else — however large the story — stays out.

Attributed, both sides shown

Every contested claim is attributed to its speaker: “Iran says X,” “US officials say Y.” When accounts conflict, both are shown side by side with confidence labels. A party's claim is never stated as fact in this page's own voice.

Severity = threat to the US

Severity measures the threat to the United States only. It is never a judgment on who is right, who started it, or whose version of events we believe.

No figure without a source

No casualty count and no dollar figure appears here without a named original source. Figures that cannot be traced to an original publisher — however widely they circulate — are not published.

Corrections — public, same day

When a report turns out wrong, it is corrected the same day, in place, with a note showing what changed — never silently deleted. This rebuild corrected three reports: the seven-day roadmap's date (Nov 11 → Sept 25, 2026), the seven conditions (now a labeled reconstruction, with the unsupported $30bn figure removed), and the word “surrender” (Rezaei's, not Ghalibaf's).

Privacy

Email addresses collected for the briefing are used for the briefing and Pro alerts only, never sold or shared, and every email can be ended with one reply.

Research alerts are not official emergency warnings. Government statements and unverified claims are labeled separately from independently confirmed events. For official alerts, see ready.gov/alerts.

All times shown in America/Chicago. Reports are grouped by their publication date as reported by their sources; because several sources publish in other time zones, a report's converted Central Time can fall on the previous evening. Sources are linked on every report and open on the original publisher. Unverified claims are flagged on the card and in the detail view.
